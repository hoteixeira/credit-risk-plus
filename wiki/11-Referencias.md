## Referências Clássicas de Risco de Crédito

### Modelos de default estruturais e reduzidos

- **Merton, R. C.** (1974). On the Pricing of Corporate Debt: The Risk Structure of Interest Rates. *Journal of Finance*, 29(2), 449–470.
- **Jarrow, R. A., Lando, D., & Turnbull, S. M.** (1997). A Markov Model for the Term Structure of Credit Risk Spreads. *Review of Financial Studies*, 10(2), 481–523.
- **Duffie, D., & Singleton, K. J.** (2003). *Credit Risk: Pricing, Measurement, and Management*. Princeton University Press.

### Modelos de portfólio de crédito

- **Gupton, G. M., Finger, C. C., & Bhatia, M.** (1997). *CreditMetrics — Technical Document*. J.P. Morgan.
- **Wilson, T.** (1997). Portfolio Credit Risk (I and II). *Risk Magazine*, 10(9-10).
- **Gordy, M. B.** (2000). A Comparative Anatomy of Credit Risk Models. *Journal of Banking & Finance*, 24(1-2), 119–149.

### Risco atuarial e distribuições de contagem

- **Johnson, N. L., Kemp, A. W., & Kotz, S.** (2005). *Univariate Discrete Distributions* (3rd ed.). Wiley.
- **Klugman, S. A., Panjer, H. H., & Willmot, G. E.** (2012). *Loss Models: From Data to Decisions* (4th ed.). Wiley.
